# Requirements: GradLink

**Defined:** 2026-05-30
**Core Value:** Graduates can prove their skills (portfolio + scored challenges) so business owners notice and hire them despite no professional track record.

## v1 Requirements

Requirements for initial release. Each maps to roadmap phases.

### Accounts

- [ ] **ACCT-01**: User can sign up and log in as either a graduate or a business owner (two distinct roles)
- [ ] **ACCT-02**: User session persists across browser refresh

### Graduate Profile

- [ ] **GRAD-01**: Graduate can create and edit a profile with name, field, bio, and skills
- [ ] **GRAD-02**: Graduate can upload portfolio items / work samples to their profile
- [ ] **GRAD-03**: Graduate can complete a platform-issued skill challenge
- [ ] **GRAD-04**: Graduate's challenge scores are displayed on their profile

### Postings

- [ ] **POST-01**: Business owner can post a job or freelance project with title, description, and required skills
- [ ] **POST-02**: Business owner can view and manage their own postings
- [ ] **POST-03**: Graduate can browse open postings
- [ ] **POST-04**: Graduate can apply to a posting

### Discovery

- [ ] **DISC-01**: Business owner can search and browse graduate profiles filtered by skill and challenge score
- [ ] **DISC-02**: Business owner can view a graduate's full profile, including portfolio and challenge scores

### Matchmaking

- [ ] **MATCH-01**: Platform recommends best-fit graduates for a posting, ranked by skill proof (challenge scores + skill match)
- [ ] **MATCH-02**: Graduate sees postings recommended to them based on their skills and scores

### Messaging

- [ ] **MSG-01**: Graduate and business owner can exchange direct messages to discuss before committing

## v2 Requirements

Deferred to future release. Tracked but not in current roadmap.

### Applications

- **APPL-01**: Business owner can track applicant status through a pipeline (applied / reviewing / accepted / rejected)
- **APPL-02**: Graduate can see the status of their applications

### Notifications

- **NOTF-01**: User receives notifications for new messages, applications, and matches
- **NOTF-02**: User can configure notification preferences

### Payments (future milestone)

- **PAY-01**: Platform processes payments for freelance projects (escrow, payouts, fees)

## Out of Scope

Explicitly excluded. Documented to prevent scope creep.

| Feature | Reason |
|---------|--------|
| On-platform payments / escrow / payouts | "Connect now, money later" — deferred to a dedicated future milestone; v1 only makes the connection |
| Contracts & invoicing | Depends on payments; handled off-platform in v1 |
| Institutional credential / degree verification | Proof of skill comes from portfolio + scored challenges in v1, not verified credentials |
| Mid-career / experienced professionals | Focus is specifically recent graduates with no track record |
| Native mobile apps | Web-first for v1 |
