## Tech debt identified

- **`eventMetId` is not indexed** on the people store. Phase 1 schema mandates all eventual fields be pre-declared, but this one slipped through. Workarounds: `db.people.toCollection().filter()` (used in `deleteEvent` cascade) and the `Set`-based hash check (used in attendee lookups). Performance impact at v1 scale (≤ thousands of rows) is negligible. **Recommendation:** add the index in a `version(2)` bump in v2 — it's an additive change.
- **`AddPersonProvider` imports `addAttendee` from the events repo**, coupling the People provider to the Events repo. Acceptable trade-off in v1 for ergonomics; cleaner alternative is a small dispatcher.
- **`useMostRecentEvent`** returns the highest-date event, which may be a future event. That's the right semantic for "what did I just attend / am about to attend" — but a recently-created future event becomes the default until something later is created.
